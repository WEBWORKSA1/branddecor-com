How to choose a branded-décor pro

  • Ask for three similar projects. A great banner printer isn't automatically a great dimensional-sign fabricator.
  • Confirm who installs. Many shops subcontract installation — ask who will be on site and whether they're insured.
  • Check permits. Exterior signs usually need a municipal permit and sometimes landlord approval. A good pro handles both.
  • Get a proof and a material sample. Approve colours on the actual material, not on a screen.
  • Agree on removal. In a leased space, ask how the work comes down and what wall repair is included.
