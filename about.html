About

Branded spaces, made simple

We help businesses turn plain walls, windows and booths into places that look and feel like their brand — without guesswork or overspending.

Why BrandDecor exists

Most small and mid-sized businesses invest heavily in a logo and website, then leave their physical space generic. The reason is rarely taste — it's uncertainty. How big should the sign be? What does a mural cost? Which material survives a busy lobby? Who actually installs it?

BrandDecor answers those questions in one place. Our calculators turn rules of thumb from the sign, print and exhibit industries into instant numbers. Our idea guides explain materials, sizes and trade-offs in plain language. Our Style Lab gives you ready-made looks to brief from. And when you're ready, one brief reaches up to three pre-screened local pros.

What we believe

  • Clarity beats decoration. A branded space should help people find their way, understand who you are and want to come back.
  • Honest numbers. We publish planning ranges and show how they're calculated, so you can walk into a quote conversation informed.
  • Clever beats expensive. Paint, film and good lighting often do more than a big custom build.
  • Your data stays yours. Calculators run in your browser. Quote briefs go only to matched pros.

How we're funded

BrandDecor is free for businesses. We earn from advertising served by Google AdSense, labelled sponsorships, optional featured directory listings and contributions from supporters. Sponsors never decide what we recommend.

Editorial standards

Guides are written and reviewed by the BrandDecor editorial team using manufacturer specifications, industry guidance and installer experience. Cost ranges are reviewed regularly and labelled as estimates. Spot an error? Tell us and we'll correct it.
