FAQ

Branded décor, answered

What is branded décor or environmental branding?

It's the use of your brand's identity — logo, colours, typography, messages and imagery — in a physical space: walls, glass, floors, signs, furniture and lighting. Done well, the space tells visitors who you are before anyone speaks.

How much does a reception logo wall cost?

For an 8-foot logo, planning ranges run roughly from a few hundred dollars for cut vinyl to several thousand for halo-lit stainless steel, plus installation. Try the visualizer for a live range.

How big should the letters on my sign be?

A widely used rule is 1 inch of letter height for every 10 feet of viewing distance for comfortable reading. Use the letter height calculator.

Is the quote service really free?

Yes, for businesses requesting quotes. BrandDecor is funded by advertising, sponsorships, optional directory listings and supporter contributions.

Can I use these ideas in a leased space?

Yes. Removable wallcovering, static-cling window film, rail-hung panels and standoff-mounted signs can all be taken down with minimal wall repair. Always check your lease.
