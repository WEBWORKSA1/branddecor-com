How to use a style recipe

  1. Start from your brand guide, not the trend. Keep your logo colours exact; use the recipe's neutrals and materials around them.
  2. Apply the 60–30–10 rule. Roughly 60% neutral wall and floor, 30% secondary material (timber, metal, fabric), 10% brand colour moments.
  3. Repeat three times. A brand colour or shape used in three places in one room reads as intentional design rather than decoration.
  4. Light it. The same sign looks twice as expensive with a wash light from above. Budget lighting with the sign, not after it.
  5. Photograph it. Check how your space looks on a phone screen — that is where most customers will see it.
