Estimates are illustrative planning ranges, not quotes. Real prices depend on your region, artwork complexity, wall type, access and permits. The preview is a simplified illustration.

How to read your result

Letter height is estimated from your sign width and the number of characters. Shorter names get taller letters at the same width. The viewing range uses the common sign-industry rule of roughly 10 feet of comfortable reading distance per inch of letter height.

The cost range multiplies an approximate sign face area by a typical per-square-foot range for the finish you chose, then adds a basic installation allowance that scales with size. Lit options (halo-lit and neon) also need a power point near the sign — budget an electrician if there isn't one.
